## 1. Game Overview & Setting

**Last War: Survival** is a mobile base-building / strategy title (developer: First Fun / Metagames Games) set in a post-apocalyptic world ravaged by a global zombie outbreak. Players take on the role of a Commander who must build and defend a base, recruit heroes ("Commanders"/survivors), train troops, and form alliances to compete for territory and resources against both zombies (PvE) and rival players (PvP).

**Story premise:** A scientist named **Noah** creates a virus that turns humans into an organized zombie force known as the **Doom Legion**. A key antagonist, referred to as the **Lady in Red**, injects herself with an enhanced version of the virus and becomes the **Crimson Queen**, leading the Crimson Legion which controls key territories with an infectious "RA virus" that mutates humans into zombies and other infected creatures.

**Seasonal story arcs** — the game world evolves through numbered seasons, each with its own antagonist, map theme, and exclusive mechanics:
- **Season 1 – The Crimson Plague:** aftermath of the Crimson Queen's rise; Crimson Legion controls key territories.
- **Season 2 – Polar Storm (Polar World):** story relocates to a frozen, formerly industrial northern region reduced to an icy wasteland; antagonist is **Emperor Boreas**, a former hero turned tyrant. Introduces a Temperature system and Rare Soil resource.
- **Season 5 – Wild West:** 8-week themed season built around Alliance influence points and individual achievements; introduces the profession system (see Section 15).
- **Season 6 (current, mid-2026):** introduces the **Hero Awakening** system (see Section 6) and continues the profession/skill-tree structure.

**Core gameplay pillars:**
- Base building & upgrading (Headquarters-gated progression)
- Resource management (Food, Iron/Steel, Gold/Coins, Gems/Diamonds)
- Troop training across 3 counter-classes and up to 11 tiers
- Hero recruitment, leveling, gear, and squad building
- Research/tech tree
- Alliance cooperation (territory, rallies, alliance wars)
- Recurring events (Arms Race, Capitol, Doomsday, Bear Hunt, Fishing, Wanted/Boss)
- PvP (world map skirmishes, alliance war, rallies) and PvE (zombie waves, monster hunts, exploration campaign)

## 2. Getting Started — First 30 Days Roadmap

### Week 1 (Days 1–7): Rush the fundamentals
- Complete every guided tutorial quest — they reward large amounts of early resources and are the fastest XP/resource source in the game.
- Build/upgrade the essentials first: **Headquarters (HQ)**, **Farm**, **Lumberyard/Iron Mine**, **Barracks**. Avoid secondary buildings unless required to raise HQ level.
- Sole priority for week 1: push **Headquarters and Barracks to level 17** as fast as possible. Don't get distracted by side systems yet.
- Level your best 3 heroes first — they carry the entire early game.
- Keep a balanced troop mix while tiers are low (roughly 40/30/30 across the three troop classes is a safe default; see Section 5).
- Join an alliance immediately. Move your base near the alliance's territory/headquarters using a free teleport. **Playing solo is a common beginner mistake** — unaffiliated bases get farmed by other players within the first 10 days.

### Days 8–14: Build momentum
- Keep rushing HQ non-stop; use speed-ups freely (they regenerate faster than most players realize, and hoarding them early is a mistake).
- Start contributing daily to alliance research and helping alliance members' construction (free, no cost to you, and it earns alliance currency).
- Begin engaging with daily systems (see Section 16 checklist) — don't skip dailies, they're your most reliable resource income.

### Days 14–30: First real squad
- Focus on building your first proper 5-hero squad (see Section 7).
- Start working toward gold-tier (Legendary) gear once the Gear Factory unlocks (HQ 9+).
- Establish a daily rhythm: dailies, radar missions, alliance helps, Arms Race participation.

### Research priority for new accounts
- Start with the **Development** and **Economy** research branches — they are effectively "free" (no combat trade-off) and their Construction/Research Speed bonuses compound on every future upgrade.
- Combat-focused research (troop attack/defense/HP) can wait until the economic foundation is in place.

### Common early mistakes to avoid
- Playing without an alliance.
- Ignoring daily tasks/quests.
- Over-investing resources into a single hero or troop tier before the account is ready to use it.
- Letting resources sit above your warehouse protection cap (they become raidable — see Section 4).
- Building secondary buildings before hitting HQ/Barracks milestones.

## 3. Base & Buildings

The base ("City"/HQ) is built from discrete building types, each gated by Headquarters level. Key structures:

- **Headquarters (HQ):** the central building; almost every other system (hero level cap, research nodes, troop tiers, building unlocks, resource-box quality) is gated behind the HQ level. Raising HQ is always the top strategic priority.
- **Barracks (multiple, per troop class):** train troops. Barracks level determines the maximum troop tier trainable (e.g., Barracks level 30 required to train Tier 10 troops).
- **Farm / Farmland:** produces Food.
- **Iron Mine / Lumberyard:** produces Iron/Steel-type resources.
- **Coin Vault:** passively generates Gold/Coins, the backbone economic resource used for upgrades, troop training, and general progression.
- **Research Lab / Tech Center:** unlocks the research tree across categories: vehicles, heroes, units, buildings, and economy — buffs ranging from reduced construction time to attack/defense boosts.
- **Gear Factory:** unlocked at HQ level 9; used to craft, upgrade, and merge hero gear (see Section 8).
- **Warehouses (Food/Iron/Gold):** protect a portion of stored resources from being plundered by attackers; upgrading warehouse capacity also grants hero XP.
- **Radar (vehicle/building):** unlocks Radar Missions and the exploration map once available.
- **Training Grounds / Armories:** support troop training and equipment logistics.

**Building strategy tips:**
- Keep resource levels below warehouse protection limits, especially before/during enemy raids.
- You can rotate your best survivors/heroes between buildings to apply their production bonuses multiple times — e.g., keep a top hero in Research Building #1 to start a research, then move them to Research Building #2 before starting the next research, capturing the bonus twice.

## 4. Resources & Economy

### Core resources
- **Food:** consumed by troop upkeep and used in building upgrades.
- **Iron / Steel:** used for building and troop upgrades.
- **Gold / Coins:** the backbone economic resource; needed for nearly every upgrade, troop training step, and progression system. Produced passively by the Coin Vault.
- **Gems / Diamonds / Crystals:** the premium currency; used to enhance weapons/gear, buy special chests, activate VIP status, and unlock high-tier upgrades. Earned free via daily quests, achievements, gathering, combat, and login rewards.

### Resource protection & management
- Upgrade warehouses (Coin Vault, Food and Iron warehouses) to raise the amount of each resource protected from plunder.
- Keep stockpiles below the protection threshold when offline or during active wars, to avoid being farmed by rival players.
- Prioritize **Farmland and Iron Mine upgrades first** in the early game to secure Food and Iron income before branching into secondary economic buildings.

### Getting resources
- Base production buildings (passive income).
- Sending troops to gather from resource tiles on the world map.
- Radar Missions, daily quests, and achievements.
- Alliance gifts/helps and alliance shop.
- Events (Arms Race, Doomsday, Capitol, Bear Hunt, Fishing, etc.).

## 5. Troops (Classes, Tiers, Training)

### The three troop classes
Last War: Survival uses a clean **rock-paper-scissors counter triangle** across three vehicle-based troop classes (not the "infantry/cavalry/archer" naming used in some other base-building games):

- **Tank:** durable frontline class. Takes **-20% damage from Missile Vehicles**, making it strong against long-range attackers.
- **Missile Vehicle:** deals **+20% damage to Aircraft** and takes reduced damage from them; strong ranged counter to air units.
- **Aircraft:** fast and agile; deals **+20%-class damage to Tanks**, excels at flanking and dodging, good at exploiting positioning weaknesses.

Counter chain: **Aircraft beats Tank → Tank beats Missile Vehicle → Missile Vehicle beats Aircraft.**

### Troop tiers (T1–T11)
- Troops are trained in ascending tiers, from T1 up to **T11** (T10 is often marketed in-game as "**Unit X**").
- **Early game:** more troops beats higher-tier troops — a larger army absorbs losses and clears tasks faster, even at lower tier. Don't rush tiers at the expense of total army size early on.
- **T9** represents the first major mid-game power spike — a substantial stat jump in attack, defense, and survivability over T8, and the most important unlock before T10 becomes reachable.
- **T10 (Unit X)** is the single biggest power jump before the pre-season/endgame content — dramatically stronger than T9 in every stat category. Training requires the relevant Barracks at **level 30**.
- **T11** is unlocked later via the Armament Institute system (endgame content).
- **Load capacity** (how many resources/troops a march can carry) scales strongly with troop tier — higher tiers carry substantially more, making tier progression the primary fix for load-capacity bottlenecks, alongside hero and building bonuses.

### Training efficiency tips
- Rather than training brand-new T10 troops from scratch, it is far more resource/time efficient to **promote existing T9 troops up to T10** — this takes a fraction of the training time for broadly similar resource cost.
- Balance your troop-class ratio according to your alliance's needs and your hero lineup's class (pure single-class squads are notably stronger — see Section 7), but a safe generalist starting ratio is roughly even across the three classes until you commit to a squad archetype.
- March size / max troop capacity is increased by: hero levels, VIP levels/perks, research, and certain unlockable heroes (e.g., a support hero unlocked at VIP 8 provides a passive march-size increase and is one of the single most impactful non-hero-leveling march-size levers).

## 6. Heroes & Commanders

Heroes (also called Commanders/Survivors) are the core of combat power, each with a rarity, a class (matching one of the three troop classes: Tank / Missile / Aircraft), and up to four skills (the fourth being an "Expertise Skill," now potentially replaced by an "Awakening Skill" — see below).

### Rarity & acquisition
- Heroes range from common tiers up to **UR (Ultra Rare)**, the highest standard rarity.
- Acquired via recruitment (using recruit tickets/currency), events, radar missions, achievements, and duplicate-shard accumulation.
- **Hero Shards:** used to unlock and star-up heroes; each hero can reach **5★**, requiring **975 shards total** to max out. Universal shards work on any hero of the matching rarity; hero-specific shards only apply to their named hero. Shards are earned from missions, events, arenas, shops, and duplicate pulls.

### Hero Awakening (Season 6 mechanic)
- Introduced in Season 6: **Hero Awakening** replaces a hero's 4th skill (previously the "Expertise Skill") with a far more powerful **Awakening Skill**, fundamentally altering the hero's base stats and combat performance and raising their damage ceiling significantly.
- Season 6 rolled out Awakening for three heroes in sequence: **Kimberly (Week 1), DVA (Week 3), Tesla (Week 5)**.

### Notable top-tier heroes (Season 6 meta, as of mid-2026)
> Hero tier lists shift with balance patches and new releases — treat this as a mid-2026 snapshot, and note the source sites for future updates.

- **Kimberly** (UR, Tank class) — considered the strongest current damage dealer in the Tank class and one of the most important heroes in the game; her tactic **Barrage Strike** fires 8 rockets, each dealing energy damage to a random enemy.
- **Murphy** (UR, Tank class) — his tactic **Ironclad Barrier** reduces the enemy front row's physical damage taken by 30% for 8 seconds; a strong early-to-mid squad tank.
- **Marshall** — buffs the entire team's attack and crit stats, maximizing squad damage output in critical moments; frequently used in Boss/Wanted-event damage setups.
- **Mason** — boosts all back-row Tank heroes' damage specifically against monsters (PvE-focused support).
- **Williams** — a frontline tank commonly anchoring "pure Tank" squad archetypes.
- **Schuyler** — crowd-control focused hero used in top-tier compositions.
- **Lucius** — noted for a weapon effect that boosts survivability across the whole team regardless of class, making him broadly useful outside of his own class squad.

### Recommended reference squads (mid-2026 meta)
- Balanced core: **Williams + Kimberly + DVA + Marshall** (+ 1 flex slot) — good all-purpose balance for most players.
- Boss/Wanted event damage testing: rotate in **Marshall** (team-wide attack/crit buff) and **Mason** (back-row Tank damage vs. monsters) and compare total damage output — the game rewards testing multiple setups per boss rather than using one fixed team everywhere.

*(Tier lists change frequently — this section should be revisited/updated each time the site is refreshed, ideally sourced from lastwarhandbook.com, lastwarvault.com, and cpt-hedge.com.)*

## 7. Squad Formations

- A squad consists of **5 heroes**, split into a **front row (2 heroes)** and a **back row (3 heroes)**.
- **Front row:** place your tankiest, most defensive heroes (shield-icon "Defenders") here — they absorb the first hits.
- **Back row:** place damage-dealing and support heroes ("Attackers") here — they deal damage and buff/debuff from behind the front line.
- **Avoid** placing pure DPS heroes in the front row — they die quickly and contribute little before falling.
- **Class purity matters:** a squad built entirely from heroes/troops of one class is measurably stronger than a mixed-class squad using the same heroes (community testing suggests roughly **+44% strength** for a pure single-class formation vs. an equivalent mixed lineup) — because troop-class bonuses and many hero skills scale with class-matched armies. Prioritize building a coherent single-class formation over chasing individually "best" heroes across different classes.

## 8. Gear & Equipment

- Gear is crafted, upgraded, and merged at the **Gear Factory**, unlocked at **HQ level 9**.
- Every hero can equip **up to 4 gear pieces**:
  - **Cannon / Railgun** — boosts attack power; best for offensive heroes.
  - **Chip** — also increases attack-related stats.
  - **Armor** — improves defense; best for tank-class heroes.
  - **Radar** — enhances defensive capabilities; protects front-line heroes.
- **Rarity tiers** (low → high): Common (green) → Blue → Epic (purple) → Legendary/UR (gold/orange). Higher rarity = bigger stat boosts.
- **Crafting example:** Legendary gear requires large amounts of crafting material (e.g., ~750 units of a material like Dielectric Ceramic), roughly 500,000 Coins, and several days of crafting time.
- **Upgrade priority:**
  - Damage-dealer heroes: **Cannon/Gun first → Radar second → Armor third → Chip fourth.**
  - Tank heroes: **Armor first → Radar second → Gun optional → Chip last.**
- **Material efficiency tip:** dismantle excess Green/Blue/Purple gear and use the reclaimed materials to merge upward into Orange (UR) gear rather than crafting UR gear from raw materials directly.
- Prioritize production of key crafting materials (Metal, Ceramics, Energy-type resources) alongside standard economic resources.

## 9. Research / Tech Tree

- Managed through the **Research Lab / Tech Center**, unlocking upgrades across five broad categories: **vehicles, heroes, units, buildings, and economy**.
- Research grants buffs such as reduced construction time and combat attack/defense boosts.
- **Early-game priority:** Development and Economy branches first (no combat trade-off, and their Construction/Research Speed bonuses compound on every subsequent upgrade); shift into combat-focused research (troop attack/defense/HP) once the economic foundation is solid.
- Multiple research queues/buildings can be used in rotation with your best hero placed in each to double-dip production/research bonuses (see Section 3 tip).

## 10. VIP System

VIP is split into two connected layers:
- **VIP Level:** permanent, based on total accumulated VIP Points (earned through daily engagement, in-game purchases, alliance participation, and specific tasks).
- **VIP Status:** a temporary activation required to actually use your current VIP level's perks — benefits are **not automatically active**; players must manually activate VIP status for a set duration, so timing activation around key building/troop pushes matters.

### Key VIP milestones (18 levels total)
- **VIP 3:** +10% construction speed.
- **VIP 6:** unlocks an extra building queue, roughly halving effective upgrade time (especially valuable during speed-up events).
- **VIP 7:** unlocks automation features that significantly cut down daily manual clicking — widely considered the practical "sweet spot" for F2P/low-spend players.
- **VIP 8:** unlocks a support survivor hero providing a passive march-size increase (see Section 5).
- **VIP 11:** construction speed reaches +50%, research speed +15%.
- **VIP 15:** +20% unit healing and +10% training speed — ideal for PvP-focused accounts.
- **VIP 18:** exclusive base cosmetic skin plus maximum prestige-tier perks.

## 11. Alliance System

Alliances are player-formed groups that cooperate on resources, defense, research, and combat, and compete against rival alliances for territory and rewards.

### Roles
- **R5 (Leader):** full control; sets alliance direction, manages diplomacy, appoints R4 officers.
- **R4 (Officer):** can accept/kick members, start rallies, manage territory.
- Lower ranks (R3–R1): general members with contribution/help permissions.

### Territory
- Captured via **Military Strongholds** and **Cities** on the alliance/warzone map.
- Each Level 1–5 City captured adds **+1 territory slot**; a Level 6 City adds **+2**.
- Territory forms connected "chains" — losing a stronghold in the middle of a chain **disconnects everything beyond it**, so expansion paths must be planned carefully.
- Staying within alliance territory grants protection against monster attacks and map curses/hazards; bases stranded outside allied territory are exposed.

### Combat & rallies
- **Rallies** are the core unit of alliance-level combat: multiple players combine troops under one commander's march to hit a target no individual player could take down alone.
- The rally leader's hero bonuses and research bonuses apply to the entire rallying force, so rally leadership should go to whoever has the strongest relevant buffs.
- **Gathering spots** are important during wars — they let troops move faster, hide marching troops from notifications, and avoid alerting the enemy when marching from far away.

### Alliance Pacts (Season 6+)
- Alliances within the same faction can form **Pact** relationships, sharing use of each other's territory and mutual battle support.
- Pact-linked alliances cannot attack each other's occupied cities.
- When attacking a city in an enemy warzone, the city is **destroyed immediately rather than captured**.

## 12. Arms Race

A recurring daily competitive event structured as follows:
- Each day is divided into **six 4-hour phases**; phase order is consistent, but exact clock times shift.
- The full daily schedule **shifts 4 hours earlier each day**, rotating fairness across time zones (Asia through the Americas over a cycle).
- Each phase focuses on a specific task category — e.g., training troops, upgrading buildings, or completing research — and players earn points by completing tasks matching the active phase.
- **Matchmaking:** every day at server midnight (00:00:00), 12–15 commanders of similar Main Base/HQ level are grouped for ranked competition based on total daily points.
- Check the in-event calendar icon (top-left of the Arms Race screen) for the live phase schedule.
- **Strategy tip:** save up actions (troop training queues, building upgrades, research starts, speed-ups) so they can be executed/completed during the matching phase, rather than spending resources during an off-phase where they generate no event points.

## 13. Server & Alliance Events

### Capitol Event
- Alliances compete to capture and hold the central **Capitol** building.
- Win condition: be the first alliance to accumulate **4 total hours** holding the Capitol, or hold the most cumulative time when the event ends.
- Surrounding the Capitol is **Contaminated Land**, which disables radar, shields, and world-exploration activity for all players in the zone — raising the stakes of any engagement nearby.
- Holding the Capitol grants a significant server-wide buff to the controlling alliance's members.

### Rampage Boss
- Extremely powerful zombie bosses periodically "awaken" and continuously attack the Capitol, emitting a rampaging aura.
- Requires the joint effort of many commanders/alliances to defeat; typically tied to the Capitol event window.

### Doomsday
- Recurring biweekly event (held every two weeks, on Sundays).
- The server is invaded by powerful **Doom Walkers**, one spawning roughly every 4 hours during the event window.
- Rewards are earned for each Doom Walker destroyed, up to **3 kills**, making it a high-value event for both individual and alliance participation.

### Bear Hunt
- A 30-minute event where alliance members jointly summon and attack a **Raging Bear**.
- Rewards scale with each participant's individual damage contribution — maximize personal damage output rather than just tapping in.

### Fishing (Season 6)
- Seasonal activity with fishing grounds, bait mechanics, and catch tiers.
- Random encounter: the **Giant Crocodile** can spawn from any catch; defeating it requires multiple coordinated rallies within roughly a 1-hour window and rewards **Stranded Fish** for all participants who contribute.

### Wanted / Big Boss Event
- A boss-damage event where players test different hero-squad setups to find their highest possible single-hit or cumulative damage output.
- Commonly recommended test heroes: **Marshall** (team-wide attack/crit buff) and **Mason** (back-row Tank damage vs. monsters).
- Community tip: don't assume one squad is optimal — actively swap heroes in/out between attempts to find your personal best-damage configuration.

### Exploration Quest (Campaign)
- The game's **permanent PvE campaign**, accessed via a dedicated "green truck" menu icon.
- Provides an ongoing, low-pressure source of resources, hero XP, and progression rewards outside of timed events.

## 14. Radar Missions & Exploration

- **Radar Missions** are daily PvE tasks unlocked once the **Radar Vehicle** is built (available after clearing a handful of early levels).
- Rewards include: Food, Coins, Iron, hero experience, in-game currency, event points, and rare items such as drone parts or recruit tickets (used for hero recruitment).
- The Radar Vehicle is what "transports" the player into the exploration/wasteland map where these missions take place.
- Stamina is consumed to run missions — plan usage around free stamina claims (see daily checklist) to avoid waste.

## 15. Seasons & Profession System

- The game restructures its content into numbered **Seasons**, each with its own map theme, antagonist, and season-exclusive mechanics (see Section 1 for story context of Seasons 1, 2, 5, 6).
- **Season 5 – Wild West:** an 8-week arc built around Alliance influence points and individual seasonal achievements; introduced the **Profession system**.
- **Profession system:** players choose between two seasonal professions — **Engineer** and **War Leader** — each with its own skill tree.
  - The right-hand column of seasonal skills is shared between both professions and **refunds all points at the end of each season**.
  - The left and middle skill-tree columns are profession-specific and remain permanent for the duration of that season (reset with the next season's rework).
- **Season 5 resource loop example:** Coffee Beans (used for seasonal building upgrades) generated by Coffee Factories, feeding progression in a "Caffeine Institute" that boosts Virus Resistance stat.
- Each new season typically resets/reworks map-specific mechanics, so seasonal guide content should be dated and periodically refreshed (this is a good candidate for a "Current Season" callout box on the site, updated per season).

## 16. Daily Checklist & F2P Strategy

### Recommended daily task checklist
- Complete all Secret/Daily tasks (Yours / Assist / Loot variants).
- Send truck deliveries/plunders to alliance members.
- Claim both free 50-stamina radar refills.
- Play 3v3 PvP: claim the 3 free "likes" for gems and aim for 5 wins.
- Clear 3 Campaign/Exploration battlefields.
- Perform 1 hero recruitment and 1 survivor recruitment.
- Complete armored truck missions.
- Claim all free loot chests: VIP chest, Mall/Hot Deals free item, event free chests.
- Get at least 1 walker (zombie) kill.
- Complete the daily "train" task.
- Run available radar tasks.
- Farm a rival player at least once (resource raid) if safe to do so.
- Buy at least 1 discounted daily/weekly pack if F2P-viable (many are gem- or resource-efficient even without spending real money, depending on currency source).

### F2P-friendly priorities
- Dailies are a reliable resource "floor" — never skip them, they compound significantly over weeks.
- VIP 7 is commonly cited as the best value stopping point for light/no-spend players due to its automation perks.
- Focus resource speed-ups and gems on Headquarters/Barracks progression and your top 3–5 heroes rather than spreading thin.
- Alliance participation (helps, research contribution, rallies) is entirely free and often the single best ROI activity in the game.

## 17. Redeem Codes

- Last War: Survival periodically releases **gift/redeem codes** granting Diamonds, Hero EXP, recruitment tickets, resource chests, speed-ups, and Hero Shards.
- Codes are typically announced around anniversaries, milestone events, and developer livestreams via the game's official social media channels.
- **Codes are time-limited** and can expire within 24–48 hours of release, so timely redemption matters.
- **Redemption process:**
  1. Copy your in-game UID (Avatar Frame → Settings → Copy ID).
  2. Go to the official Last War Code Redemption Center (in-browser).
  3. Log in, enter the code, and submit.
  4. Collect the reward from your in-game Mailbox.
- Since codes rotate constantly, the site should maintain a **live/updatable "Active Codes" section** rather than hardcoding specific codes into static manual content — pull from tracking sites (e.g., lastwarhandbook.com/codes) periodically.
